# Shania Legal

> Spanish and EU legal research with cited articles and a citation checker that does not rely on an AI model.

## What it is

Shania Legal answers legal questions with the applicable law quoted by article, and checks that what a draft cites really exists and says what is claimed. It is built on a consolidated corpus of Spanish and European legislation with article, validity date and ELI identifier. It is an assistant for professionals, not a substitute for legal advice.

## Who it is for

- Real-estate agencies that handle leases, sales, mortgages and urban-planning questions.
- Legal and administrative professionals who need quick, cited references.
- Teams that want to check citations in a document before it goes out.

## What it does

- Search by exact citation (returns the literal article) and by text, with the current version of each norm.
- Answers with the law cited and a citation judge that flags unsupported references.
- Citation verifier: deterministic, no AI model involved in the check. A law it does not have is reported as "unchecked", never as "wrong".
- Contract review and comparison (lexical: it says "does not appear", never "is false").
- Preparation of court filings in the electronic judicial document format, with LexNET filing types and size and format limits. It prepares the document; it does not file it.
- Legislation library covering civil, tenancy, property, mortgage, consumer, procedure, criminal, tax, data protection, urban planning and housing law, among others.

## Status

**In production as a CRM module; standalone web version in development**

Available inside Shania CRM as a module enabled per subject area. A standalone web product is planned: today only the engine exists (no standalone interface, accounts or multi-tenant support). Case-law search is planned.

## Important

Shania Legal does not give legal advice and does not replace a lawyer. Always check important decisions with a qualified professional.
